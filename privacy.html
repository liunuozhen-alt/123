糯叽机 · 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-05-31

感谢你使用糯叽机（Nuojiji，下称「本应用」）。本应用是一款本地优先的虚构 AI 角色扮演聊天应用，仅供年满 18 周岁的用户使用。我们高度重视你的隐私。请仔细阅读以下说明。

一、本应用如何处理你的数据

1. 数据本地存储

你在本应用中创建的几乎所有内容——包括 AI 角色、聊天记录、朋友圈、世界书、设置、图片等——都仅存储在你自己设备的本地存储（IndexedDB / localStorage）中。这些数据不会上传到我们的服务器，我们也无法查看。卸载应用或清除数据将永久删除它们，请自行做好备份。

2. 关于 AI 内容生成（重要）

本应用不自带、不托管任何 AI 服务，也不内置用于聊天的 API 密钥。要使用 AI 聊天功能，你必须在设置中填入你自己的第三方 AI 服务（如 Anthropic、OpenAI 等）的 API 密钥。

  • 你的聊天内容和密钥仅会发送到你自己选择的那个 AI 服务商，由该服务商处理，受其各自的隐私政策与使用条款约束。
  • 本应用仅作为「前端客户端」转发请求，不存储、不上传、不分析你的聊天内容。
  • 所有 AI 生成的内容均为虚构，由你自定义的角色设定与提示词驱动，其内容由你与所选 AI 服务商共同决定，开发者对生成结果不承担责任。

3. 账户与登录

本应用使用第三方身份验证服务（Supabase / PocketBase）进行登录，仅用于账户验证。此过程可能涉及你的登录邮箱和一个匿名设备标识符，用于区分设备与防止滥用。我们不会将其用于广告或出售给第三方。

4. 设备权限

  • 麦克风：仅在你使用语音输入 / 语音消息 / 视频通话功能时使用。
  • 相机：仅在你使用视频通话 / 拍照功能时使用。
  • 相册：仅在你设置头像、发送图片、导入 / 导出备份时使用。

以上权限均在你主动使用对应功能时才请求，数据不离开你的设备（除非你主动发送给所选 AI 服务）。

二、我们不收集什么

我们不收集你的聊天内容、角色设定、媒体文件，也不进行用户画像或定向广告。

三、面向成年人

本应用为成人向虚构创作工具，仅供年满 18 周岁的用户使用。首次启动时会要求你确认年龄。请勿让未成年人使用。

四、内容与举报

由于内容由用户自建 AI 在本地生成，开发者无法实时审查。若你认为某段 AI 生成内容不当，可通过应用内「设置 → 内容举报 / 反馈」联系开发者，我们会据此改进内容安全策略。

五、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 18 岁以下人群，我们不会有意收集未成年人的任何信息。

六、政策变更

本政策如有更新，将在此页面公布并更新「最后更新」日期。

Nuojiji · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-31

Thank you for using Nuojiji (the "App"). The App is a local-first, fiction-only AI role-play chat application, intended solely for users aged 18 and over. We take your privacy seriously. Please read the following carefully.

1. How the App Handles Your Data

1.1 Local Storage

Almost everything you create in the App — AI characters, chat history, moments, world books, settings, images, etc. — is stored only in your device's local storage (IndexedDB / localStorage). This data is never uploaded to our servers, and we cannot view it. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data will permanently delete it, so please keep your own backups.

1.2 AI Content Generation (Important)

The App does not include or host any AI service, and contains no built-in API key for chat. To use AI chat features, you must enter your own API key for a third-party AI service (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI) in the settings.

  • Your chat content and key are sent only to the AI provider you choose, processed by that provider, and governed by that provider's own privacy policy and terms.
  • The App acts solely as a front-end client that forwards requests; it does not store, upload, or analyze your chat content.
  • All AI-generated content is fictional, driven by character settings and prompts you define. Its content is determined jointly by you and your chosen AI provider; the developer is not responsible for generated output.

1.3 Account & Sign-in

The App uses a third-party authentication service (Supabase / PocketBase) for sign-in, used only for account verification. This may involve your sign-in email and an anonymous device identifier to distinguish devices and prevent abuse. We do not use it for advertising or sell it to third parties.

1.4 Device Permissions

  • Microphone: only for voice input / voice messages / video calls.
  • Camera: only for video calls / taking photos.
  • Photos: only for setting avatars, sending images, and importing / exporting backups.

These permissions are requested only when you actively use the corresponding feature, and data does not leave your device (unless you actively send it to your chosen AI service).

2. What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your chat content, character settings, or media files, and we do not perform user profiling or targeted advertising.

3. For Adults Only

The App is an adult-oriented fictional creation tool, intended solely for users aged 18 and over. You will be asked to confirm your age on first launch. Do not allow minors to use it.

4. Content & Reporting

Because content is generated locally by user-created AI, the developer cannot review it in real time. If you find any AI-generated content inappropriate, you can contact the developer via "Settings → Content Report / Feedback" in the App, which helps us improve content safety.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect any information from minors.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page and revise the "Last updated" date.
